Privacy Policy

Never Have I Ever · 7 October 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy describes how the iPhone app Never Have I Ever handles information. The game has no account, and the developer does not receive player names or other game data on a server.

The game is for adults, 18 and over, and is not directed at children.

2. What stays on the phone

The app can remember on the phone:

  • names the players type, so the same names can be offered next time;
  • the language chosen in the app.

That information is used only to run the game. It stays on the device, is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, is not sold, and is removed when the app is deleted.

Answers, votes, and the cards of the current round are not saved.

3. The phone

On first launch the app reads the language of the phone so it can open in that language when a translation exists. That reading stays on the phone. The app does not collect location, contacts, photos, messages, or an advertising identifier.

If the system rating sheet appears, Apple shows it. The rating goes to Apple. The developer does not receive the player's name with that rating.

4. Advertising and analytics

The app has no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking services.

5. The paid subscription

Weekly access is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and keeps the purchase record. The developer does not receive the card number or other payment details. The app only asks Apple whether the subscription is active.

6. Security

Game data does not leave the phone, so keeping it safe depends on the protection of the device itself.

7. Changes to this policy

The developer may update this page. The date at the top is the date of the current text. The published page is the current policy.
